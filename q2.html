Welcome to Kyoto

Kyoto blends centuries of heritage with modern life, from quiet temples to bustling streets lined with cherry blossoms.

Culture & Traditions

Geisha performances, tea ceremonies, and traditional crafts like pottery and weaving thrive in this ancient capital.

Temples & Gardens

Kyoto is home to over 1600 Buddhist temples and 400 Shinto shrines, each surrounded by tranquil gardens that shift with the seasons.

Delicious Cuisine

From kaiseki multi-course dining to comforting bowls of ramen, Kyoto’s food culture is rich in flavor and artistry.
